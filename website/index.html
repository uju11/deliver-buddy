Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Delivery Buddy safe to install?

Yes, 100% safe. Since it is distributed directly as an APK rather than the Google Play Store, Android shows a standard warning for sideloaded apps. The app contains zero malware or trackers.

How does the Floating Buddy overlay work with Ekart Field X?

The Floating Buddy runs as a lightweight overlay service. When enabled, it displays a small floating icon on your screen that you can tap while using Ekart Field X to quickly view customer details, make calls, or check delivery notes without switching apps.

Does it require an internet connection?

Delivery Buddy is designed with an offline-first architecture. Runsheet parsing, stop reordering, and fuel calculations work entirely offline. Internet is only used when opening map navigation or searching online petrol pumps.
